How to play

THE GOAL

Score the most points over all volleys. There are no hit points: every blast scores based on weapon and how close it lands to the enemy tank. Use every weapon in your arsenal; each is single-use.

DRAFT

You and the opponent take turns picking weapons from the shared pool. Picks are unique, so grab the good ones first.

AIMING

←→ rotate the barrel a full 360° (you can aim down too), ↑↓ set power. Hold Shift for fine control. Or drag with mouse/touch from near your tank. Fire with Space.

MOVING

Hold A / D to drive your tank. Driving burns fuel, and your fuel refills every turn.

WEAPONS

Click a card or press 1–9, 0. Tab cycles. Hover cards for details.

THE WORLD

Terrain is fully destructible and shields blasts. Wind pushes shells (not lasers). Tanks that get buried dig themselves out at the next turn.

TRAPS, PAINT & TERRAIN

Quicksand and Glue leave patches that trap tanks for a few volleys. Bouncy Paint coats the ground: shells ricochet and tanks bounce. Mountain builds a peak (land it near the enemy and it rises under them to bury them), Mountain Remover shaves everything above its impact height, Digger drills a deep shaft.

TANKS & PAINT

Pick a tank model and colour on the title screen. Buy more models (each with a perk) and paint finishes in the SHOP.
